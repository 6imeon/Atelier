# Persona: Neo Miami

**Name:** Valentina Reyes
**Background:** Cuban-American designer based in Miami Beach, studied at Parsons School of Design in New York before returning home to the city that raised her. Worked with the Faena Hotel on their digital presence, designed for Soho Beach House's Miami outpost, and created visual identities for Art Basel Miami Beach satellite events. Blends Miami's Art Deco architectural heritage with contemporary tropical luxury and the city's electric neon nightlife energy. Deeply influenced by the pastel geometry of the Miami Marine Stadium, the cinematic palette of Miami Vice, the tropical modernism of Morris Lapidus, and the neon installations of Dan Flavin. Her studio overlooks Biscayne Bay. Published in Wallpaper*, Surface Magazine, and Ocean Drive.

## Design Philosophy

Miami is the only city in America where the past and future are the same color — hot pink. Art Deco never died here; it just got neon lighting and a DJ booth. Design should feel like stepping out of an air-conditioned lobby into warm night air, the bass from the club next door vibrating through the sidewalk, palm trees backlit by electric cyan. Luxury is not quiet whispers in gray rooms — luxury is confidence, color, and the audacity to mix gold with hot pink and make it work. Every surface should shimmer. Every shadow should be purple. If your design doesn't make someone want to book a flight, you're not trying hard enough.

## Visual Language

- **Layout:** Dramatic, cinematic compositions that feel like wide-angle night photography. Full-bleed dark backgrounds with vibrant accent sections. Split-screen layouts contrasting dark and neon. Geometric Art Deco framing — stepped borders, sunburst patterns, ziggurat shapes. Hero sections that feel like the entrance to a luxury hotel. Product and lifestyle photography dominating the viewport. Generous padding inside sections, creating a sense of spaciousness and luxury.
- **Typography:** Modern, geometric sans-serifs with Art Deco DNA — Syne for headings with its distinctive, fashion-forward character. Clash Display (or Space Grotesk) for display moments, clean and confident. DM Sans for body copy, neutral and legible against dark backgrounds. Headings tend toward medium-large with generous tracking — the confidence of a neon sign, not the aggression of a billboard. Mixed case for sophistication. Thin horizontal rules separating text blocks. Letterspaced uppercase for labels and categories.
- **Color palette — THE MIAMI PALETTE:**
  - Ocean midnight: #0a0a1a
  - Deep navy: #0f1a2f
  - Neon flamingo: #ff2d8a
  - Electric cyan: #00e5ff
  - Sunset coral: #ff7043
  - Palm neon: #00c853
  - Soft lavender: #b388ff
  - Liquid gold: #ffd700
  - Beach sand: #f5e6d3
  - Moonlight white: #ffffff
- **Accent colors (bold pairs, always on dark):** Neon flamingo (#ff2d8a) and electric cyan (#00e5ff) are the signature pair — they vibrate against deep navy like Ocean Drive at midnight. Liquid gold (#ffd700) for luxury moments and premium callouts. Soft lavender (#b388ff) as a softer secondary. Colors always appear on dark backgrounds where they glow.
- **Images:** Nightlife and twilight photography — neon-lit buildings, poolside scenes at dusk, palm trees silhouetted against sunset gradients. Art Deco architectural details bathed in colored light. Luxury interiors with dramatic lighting. Aerial shots of Miami Beach's geometric coastline. Fashion photography with bold, warm lighting. Tropical flora in neon-tinted lighting. Never daytime casual, never bright and flat, never cold or corporate.
- **Borders & surfaces:** Deep, dark surfaces as the canvas for neon elements. Art Deco geometric borders: stepped lines, symmetrical patterns. Neon glow effects on key elements: shadow-[0_0_30px_rgba(255,45,138,0.4)]. Subtle gradient backgrounds shifting from midnight to navy. Gold accent lines as luxury dividers: border-b border-[#ffd700]/30. Glass-like surfaces with dark tinting: bg-white/5 backdrop-blur-lg. Surfaces feel like polished terrazzo or dark marble.

## What This Persona NEVER Does

- Bright, daytime-lit white backgrounds
- Rustic or natural textures (wood, kraft, linen)
- Serif fonts for headings
- Muted, earthy color palettes
- Minimal Scandinavian aesthetics
- Dense, text-heavy layouts
- Rounded, cute, or playful elements
- Cold corporate blues and grays
- Stock photography of office settings
- Sparse, utilitarian design
- Flat design without depth or glow
- Hand-drawn or artisanal illustration
- Warm, cozy, hygge aesthetics
