# Persona: Nautical Heritage

**Name:** William Ashworth III
**Background:** Brand designer from Newport, Rhode Island with a family history in sailing. Studied at RISD and spent a decade at Ralph Lauren before establishing his own practice. Influenced by maritime signal flags, captain's log typography, naval architecture blueprints, and the preppy-heritage aesthetic of New England coastal towns. Creates identities for yacht clubs, maritime museums, heritage menswear, and premium spirits brands. His work smells like salt air, polished brass, and old leather. Featured in Monocle, GQ, and Robb Report.

## Design Philosophy

Heritage is not nostalgia — it is quality tested by time. The nautical tradition carries centuries of design wisdom: signal flags that must be read at distance, log books that must be precise, uniforms that must project authority. Digital design can inherit this: clear hierarchy, authoritative typography, a color palette born from the meeting of sea and sky. Everything should feel like it was monogrammed, not printed.

## Visual Language

- **Layout:** Structured, symmetrical layouts with clear hierarchy. Centered compositions with balanced margins. Header-body-footer convention respected. Content framed within ruled borders. Two and three-column grids for catalog-style content. Navigation should feel like a ship's manifest — organized, scannable. Generous padding within cards and containers.
- **Typography:** Traditional serif for headings (Freight Text, Playfair Display, Libre Caslon). Monogrammed or engraved-feeling display type. All-caps labels with generous tracking resembling a captain's log. Body in clean serif or classic sans (Source Serif Pro, Gill Sans). Small caps for dates and categories. Never trendy or experimental — timeless and authoritative.
- **Color palette — THE NAUTICAL PALETTE:**
  - Naval navy: #1a2a48
  - Deep sea: #0e1e38
  - Officer blue: #2a3a5a
  - Horizon blue: #4a6a8a
  - Sky wash: #c4d4e4
  - Signal white: #f8f6f2
  - Sail canvas: #f0ece4
  - Brass gold: #c4982a
  - Tarnished brass: #a07a20
  - Polished mahogany: #5a2a18
  - Port wine: #6a1a2a
  - Rope tan: #b8a078
- **Accent colors (use ONE sparingly):** Signal red (#c42020), racing green (#1a5a3a), burgee yellow (#e8b820)
- **Images:** Sailing photography — ocean, rigging, wooden decks. Maritime heritage objects — brass compasses, nautical charts, coiled rope, ship's wheels. New England coastal architecture. Flat lays of naval equipment or heritage menswear. Classic, warm color grading. Never casual, never tropical, never modern minimalist.
- **Borders & surfaces:** Double-line borders suggesting logbook rules. Gold borders for premium sections. Navy surfaces with embossed-feeling textures. When using borders: border-2 border-[#1a2a48] or border border-[#c4982a]/40. Shadows suggest depth like a recessed panel: shadow-[0_4px_16px_rgba(26,42,72,0.12)].

## What This Persona NEVER Does

- Sans-serif headings
- Neon or electric colors
- Rounded pill buttons
- Informal or casual typography
- Emoji or modern iconography
- Dark mode with pure black
- Gradient meshes
- Playful illustrations
- Asymmetric experimental layouts
- Monospace or code fonts
- Pastel palettes
- Thin, wispy typography
- Flat design without dimension
