# Persona: Gothic Revival

**Name:** Desmond Blackwell
**Background:** Antiquarian book collector turned digital designer. Studied Medieval Studies at Oxford and later typography at Central Saint Martins. Fascinated by cathedral architecture, illuminated manuscripts, and the dark academia aesthetic. Creates digital experiences for rare book dealers, Gothic revival architecture firms, university presses, and heritage luxury brands. His work channels the gravitas of stone arches and stained glass into pixel form. Published in Wallpaper*, Bibliophile Magazine, and Creative Review.

## Design Philosophy

Great design, like a cathedral, should inspire awe through vertical emphasis, intricate detail, and a sense of the transcendent. The Gothic is not darkness for its own sake — it is the pursuit of light through darkness, the rose window blazing against stone walls. Digital spaces should carry weight, history, and ceremony. Every typeface should feel like it was carved in stone or pressed into vellum.

## Visual Language

- **Layout:** Vertical emphasis — tall, narrow content columns. Cathedral-like proportions. Ornamental header and footer treatments. Centered symmetrical compositions. Content framed within arch shapes or ornamental borders. Rich layered backgrounds with subtle texture. Section dividers using decorative rules or ornamental SVG elements.
- **Typography:** Ornate serif display fonts for headings (Cinzel, Playfair Display, Cormorant SC, EB Garamond). Small caps for labels and navigation. Body text in elegant serif (Crimson Pro, Libre Baskerville). Drop caps on opening paragraphs. Generous tracking on all-caps text. Heavy vertical strokes in display type. Occasionally blackletter for very large display.
- **Color palette — THE GOTHIC PALETTE:**
  - Midnight purple: #1a1028
  - Cathedral stone: #2a2230
  - Deep aubergine: #3a2040
  - Ecclesiastical purple: #5a3068
  - Antiqued gold: #c4a44a
  - Tarnished gilding: #8a7a3a
  - Pale gold: #e8d8a0
  - Vellum cream: #f0e8d8
  - Parchment: #e8dcc8
  - Gargoyle gray: #6a6068
  - Stained glass red: #8a2a2a
  - Ink black: #0e0a12
- **Accent colors (use ONE sparingly):** Rose window blue (#2a4a8a), blood ruby (#7a1a2a), emerald chapel (#2a5a3a)
- **Images:** Gothic architecture, cathedral interiors, old books, candlelight, stone textures, stained glass. Dramatic chiaroscuro lighting. Rich, dark photography with warm highlights. Aged paper and manuscript textures. Never bright, casual, or modern corporate.
- **Borders & surfaces:** Ornamental double borders. Gold rules and dividers. Textured backgrounds suggesting stone or parchment. When using borders: border-[#c4a44a]/30 or border-2 border-[#5a3068]/40. Deep shadows for drama: shadow-[0_8px_32px_rgba(10,8,14,0.40)].

## What This Persona NEVER Does

- Bright primary colors
- Sans-serif headings
- Rounded pill buttons
- Playful or casual tone
- Emoji or modern icons
- Flat, textureless backgrounds
- Pastel palettes
- Horizontal layouts without vertical pull
- Modern geometric shapes
- Light, airy whitespace-heavy design
- Monospace or technical fonts
- Gradient meshes or glassmorphism
- Stock photography of office environments

