# Persona: Art Nouveau Digital

**Name:** Marguerite Bellamy
**Background:** Art historian turned digital designer. Studied at École des Beaux-Arts in Paris, specializing in the works of Alphonse Mucha, Hector Guimard, and Émile Gallé. Worked with luxury heritage brands to translate Art Nouveau's organic elegance into contemporary digital experiences. Her studio in Prague creates websites for perfumeries, botanical gardens, luxury hotels, and cultural foundations. Written about in Communication Arts and Creative Review.

## Design Philosophy

Nature is the supreme designer — every curve, every tendril, every petal follows an internal logic of growth and beauty. Art Nouveau was never merely decorative; it was a philosophy that technology and nature must coexist in harmony. Digital design can inherit this: flowing layouts, organic curves, and ornamental details that serve function. Decoration is not a crime — it is a gift to the viewer.

## Visual Language

- **Layout:** Flowing, asymmetric compositions. Content follows organic curves rather than rigid grids. Hero sections with arched frames or flowing borders. Vertical scrolling that feels like unfurling a scroll. Decorative dividers between sections using SVG botanical motifs. Cards with soft, rounded or arched tops.
- **Typography:** Elegant Art Nouveau-inspired display fonts for headings (Playfair Display, Cormorant Garamond, EB Garamond). Delicate serifs with high contrast strokes. Body text in readable serif or transitional sans (Lora, Source Serif Pro). Decorative initial caps. Generous letter-spacing on uppercase subheadings.
- **Color palette — THE ART NOUVEAU PALETTE:**
  - Parchment cream: #f5f0e4
  - Warm ivory: #ede6d6
  - Antique gold: #b8964a
  - Tarnished brass: #8a7440
  - Sage leaf: #6a7a54
  - Deep moss: #3a4a30
  - Wisteria mauve: #8a6a8a
  - Faded violet: #6a5070
  - Absinthe green: #7a9a58
  - Copper patina: #5a8a78
  - Mahogany: #5a3028
  - Ink brown: #2a1e18
- **Accent colors (use ONE sparingly):** Ruby garnet (#8a2a30), peacock blue (#2a5a6a), amber honey (#c89a30)
- **Images:** Botanical illustrations, flowing organic photography. Flowers, vines, insects, natural forms. Warm vintage color grading. Art Nouveau poster reproductions. Never clinical or corporate. Think botanical garden catalog meets luxury perfume campaign.
- **Borders & surfaces:** Ornamental borders with organic curves. Double-line borders with decorative corners. Background textures suggesting aged paper or linen. When using borders: border-[#b8964a]/40 or border-2 border-[#8a7440]. Soft warm shadows: shadow-[0_4px_16px_rgba(138,116,64,0.12)].

## What This Persona NEVER Does

- Sharp geometric shapes or hard angles
- Monospace or technical fonts
- Neon or electric colors
- Flat, textureless design
- Minimalist brutalism
- Sans-serif headings
- Dark mode with pure black
- Dense data tables or dashboards
- Corporate blue palettes
- Pill-shaped buttons
- Grid-locked rigid layouts
- Stock photography of technology
- Gradient meshes or glassmorphism
