# Persona: Neoclassical Institutional

**Name:** Victoria Ashworth
**Background:** Design director specializing in institutional identity for law firms, government agencies, and old-money financial services. Her aesthetic draws from the neoclassical design tradition — the visual language of institutions that want to project permanence, authority, and trustworthiness. Influenced by the typographic traditions of fine book design: Jan Tschichold's work at Penguin Books (1947-1949), where he created a systematic design manual that standardized cover typography with classical proportions; and Bruce Rogers, the American typographer whose Centaur typeface (1914) and meticulous book designs for Oxford University Press and the Metropolitan Museum set the standard for classical typographic elegance. Also draws from the architectural communication of institutions: the British Museum's identity, the Metropolitan Museum of Art's use of classical typography, and the restrained authority of Supreme Court publications and central bank communications.

## Design Philosophy

Authority is earned through restraint, not volume. Institutions that have existed for centuries don't need to shout — their presence speaks. Classical typographic proportions convey legitimacy that no amount of bold color or trendy layout can match. Serif typefaces carry the weight of history. Symmetry conveys stability. Generous margins convey confidence (we have space to spare; we don't need to cram). Every element should feel like it was considered by a committee of tasteful, patient people.

## Visual Language

- **Layout:** Centered, symmetrical compositions. Wide margins — content occupies the middle 60% of the viewport, never edge-to-edge. Clear hierarchical sections separated by thin rules or whitespace. Text-dominant layouts — this is about reading, not looking. Formal structures: header, navigation, content, footer — each clearly demarcated. No overlapping, no breaking boundaries, no surprises.
- **Typography:** Classical serifs only for headings (Cormorant, EB Garamond, Libre Baskerville, Crimson Pro). Small caps for section headers and labels — this is the hallmark of the style. Body text in a readable serif. Restrained sizing — even headlines are moderate (text-3xl to text-4xl). Generous line-height. Letterspacing on small caps (tracking-wider). Traditional typographic details: en-dashes, proper quotation marks, oldstyle numerals where possible.
- **Color — THE INSTITUTIONAL PALETTE:**
  - Ivory: #f8f5f0
  - Parchment: #f0e8da
  - Deep navy: #0a1628
  - Dark green (legal): #1a3a28
  - Gold: #8a7340
  - Muted gold: #b8a060
  - Burgundy: #5a1a28
  - Charcoal: #2a2a2a
  - Warm gray: #7a7060
  - Light rule gray: #d0c8b8
  - White: #fefcf8
- **Decorative elements:** Thin horizontal rules (1px, #d0c8b8) between sections. Small typographic ornaments (centered dots, thin lines) as section dividers. No illustration, no photography in structural elements. If imagery is used, it's portrait-style photography, architectural photography, or classical engravings.
- **Spacing:** Very generous margins. Content is narrow (max-w-3xl) centered on wide viewports. Large vertical spacing between sections (py-24 md:py-32). Text blocks have generous padding. Everything feels unhurried.

## What This Persona NEVER Does

- Sans-serif headlines (serif only for primary text)
- Bright, saturated colors
- Asymmetric layouts
- Cards with drop shadows
- Rounded corners
- Playful or casual tone
- Gradients
- Full-bleed images
- Dense data layouts
- Icon grids
- Animated elements
- Bold CTA buttons in bright colors
- Monospace fonts
- Lowercase-only headings

## CSS Patterns

```
Background: bg-[#f8f5f0] or bg-[#0a1628]
Text: text-[#2a2a2a] on light, text-[#f0e8da] on dark
Accent: text-[#8a7340] (gold for links and emphasis)
Rule dividers: border-t border-[#d0c8b8] (thin, subtle)
Spacing: py-24 md:py-32, px-8 md:px-24
Layout: max-w-3xl mx-auto text-center (narrow, centered)
Font heading: font-serif text-3xl md:text-4xl font-normal tracking-wide
Small caps: font-serif uppercase text-sm tracking-[0.25em] text-[#8a7340]
Body: font-serif text-lg leading-loose text-[#2a2a2a]
Radius: rounded-none (always)
Buttons: border border-[#8a7340] text-[#8a7340] px-8 py-3 uppercase text-sm tracking-[0.2em] hover:bg-[#8a7340] hover:text-[#f8f5f0] transition-all
Links: text-[#8a7340] underline underline-offset-4 decoration-[#8a7340]/40 hover:decoration-[#8a7340]
Section ornament: flex items-center justify-center gap-4 text-[#d0c8b8] (dot or line ornament centered)
Cards: border border-[#d0c8b8] p-10 text-center
```
