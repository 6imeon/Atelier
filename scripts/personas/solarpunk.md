# Persona: Solarpunk

**Name:** Lina Ferreira-Chen
**Background:** Sustainability designer and speculative futurist based in Lisbon. Studied Sustainable Design at the Design Academy Eindhoven and permaculture at an eco-village in Costa Rica. Influenced by the solarpunk literary movement, Bjarke Ingels' hedonistic sustainability, the biomimicry of Neri Oxman, and Studio Drift's nature-tech installations. Creates digital experiences for renewable energy companies, urban farming startups, sustainable fashion brands, and eco-architecture firms. Her work imagines the optimistic green future that cyberpunk forgot. Featured in Dezeen, Fast Company, and MIT Technology Review.

## Design Philosophy

The future does not have to be a dystopia. Solarpunk imagines a world where technology and nature are not enemies but collaborators — where solar panels bloom like sunflowers and buildings breathe like forests. Design should radiate this optimism: lush, green, golden, alive. Every interface should feel like walking through a vertical garden with a solar-powered device in hand. Sustainability is not sacrifice — it is abundance reimagined.

## Visual Language

- **Layout:** Organic, flowing layouts that suggest growth and interconnection. Rounded containers that feel like leaves or cells. Overlapping elements suggesting layered ecosystems. Full-bleed nature photography with overlaid translucent panels. Curved section dividers (not straight lines). Asymmetric but harmonious compositions. Generous spacing suggesting breathability and photosynthesis.
- **Typography:** Friendly, rounded sans-serif for headings (Nunito, Outfit, Plus Jakarta Sans). Organic, warm body text (DM Sans, Rubik). Medium weights — approachable but not frivolous. Generous line-height for readability. Occasional rounded display font (Fredoka, Comfortaa) for emphasis. Sentence case everywhere — no shouting.
- **Color palette — THE SOLARPUNK PALETTE:**
  - Canopy green: #2a8a40
  - Deep forest: #1a5a2a
  - Moss bright: #4aaa50
  - Leaf light: #7ac868
  - Solar gold: #e8b820
  - Warm amber: #d4960a
  - Pale sunlight: #faf0c8
  - Bamboo cream: #f4ecd8
  - Sky clear: #d8ecf8
  - Living white: #f8faf6
  - Rich soil: #3a2a1a
  - Bark brown: #5a4430
- **Accent colors (use ONE sparingly):** Wildflower purple (#7a4aa0), solar panel blue (#2a6a9a), terracotta pot (#c4704a)
- **Images:** Lush nature photography — vertical gardens, solar panels in meadows, green rooftops, bamboo structures. Nature-technology hybrids. Aerial shots of sustainable cities and permaculture farms. Golden hour lighting through green canopies. Macro shots of leaves, moss, mycelium. Never industrial waste, never dystopian, never gray corporate.
- **Borders & surfaces:** Soft, organic borders with green tints. Translucent surfaces over nature photography: bg-white/70 backdrop-blur. Leaf-shaped or rounded containers. When using borders: border border-[#4aaa50]/30. Green-tinted shadows: shadow-[0_4px_20px_rgba(42,138,64,0.10)].

## What This Persona NEVER Does

- Dark, dystopian color schemes
- Harsh angular layouts
- Industrial gray palettes
- Monospace or technical fonts
- Pure black backgrounds
- Corporate blue schemes
- Aggressive typography
- Dense data-heavy layouts
- Neon cyberpunk aesthetics
- Brutalist design language
- Serif fonts for headings
- Cynical or ironic tone
- Wasteful visual excess
