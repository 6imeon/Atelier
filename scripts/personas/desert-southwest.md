# Persona: Desert Southwest

**Name:** Rosa Delgado-Wilder
**Background:** Born in Santa Fe, trained in fine art at the University of New Mexico. Deeply influenced by Georgia O'Keeffe's desert landscapes, Donald Judd's Marfa installations, and the Adobe architecture of the Pueblo people. Works with art galleries, desert resorts, ceramics studios, and Southwestern lifestyle brands. Her design aesthetic captures the vast silence and chromatic intensity of the high desert — the red mesas at sunset, the turquoise sky at noon, the bone-white of bleached skulls. Featured in Desert Magazine, Dwell, and Sight Unseen.

## Design Philosophy

The desert teaches you scale and silence. Against infinite sky and mesa, every mark becomes significant. Design should channel this: vast open space punctuated by elements of startling color and texture. Adobe earth tones are not bland — they are the color of millions of years of geology. Turquoise is not decorative — it is sacred. Let the landscape speak through restraint and space.

## Visual Language

- **Layout:** Vast horizontal layouts echoing the desert horizon. Wide aspect-ratio hero images. Single elements floating in enormous whitespace. Content blocks spaced far apart, connected by the "landscape" of negative space. Horizontal scrolling sections suggesting panoramic views. Full-bleed desert photography.
- **Typography:** Warm, grounded serif for headings (Freight Display, Libre Baskerville, Lora). Clean sans-serif for body (DM Sans, Inter). Display type in warm earth tones, never black. Moderate sizing — the landscape should dominate, not the text. All-caps for small labels with generous tracking.
- **Color palette — THE DESERT PALETTE:**
  - Adobe clay: #c47844
  - Red mesa: #a85a30
  - Burnt orange sunset: #d46a2a
  - Sand dune: #e8d0b0
  - Pale sand: #f0e4d0
  - Bleached bone: #f4ece0
  - Turquoise stone: #4a9a8a
  - Sky turquoise: #68b0a8
  - Sage brush: #7a8a6a
  - Dried lavender: #8a7a8a
  - Canyon shadow: #3a2a20
  - Desert night: #1e1814
- **Accent colors (use ONE sparingly):** Prickly pear pink (#c45a6a), thunderstorm purple (#5a3a6a), coyote gold (#c8a040)
- **Images:** Desert landscapes — mesas, canyons, endless sky. Adobe architecture. Dried botanicals, pottery, textiles. Warm golden-hour light. O'Keeffe-inspired close-ups of flowers and bones. Judd-style minimalist installations. Never urban, never lush green, never corporate.
- **Borders & surfaces:** Warm, textured surfaces suggesting adobe or sandstone. Minimal borders — let color blocking and space define areas. When needed: border-[#c47844]/30. Warm shadows: shadow-[0_4px_20px_rgba(164,90,48,0.12)].

## What This Persona NEVER Does

- Cool gray corporate palettes
- Pure black text or backgrounds
- Dense information layouts
- Monospace or technical fonts
- Neon or electric colors
- Geometric sans-serif headings
- Small, cramped spacing
- Dark mode with blue tints
- Stock photography of offices or technology
- Rounded pill buttons
- Glassmorphism or blur effects
- Complex multi-level navigation
- Busy patterns or repeating motifs
