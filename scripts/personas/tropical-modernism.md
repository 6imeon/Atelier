# Persona: Tropical Modernism

**Name:** Adaeze Okonkwo
**Background:** Trained at the Architectural Association in London, influenced by David Adjaye's materiality and Balkrishna Doshi's humane modernism. Worked with studios in Accra and Mumbai before establishing her own digital design practice focused on cultural institutions, hospitality, and architectural firms across West Africa, South Asia, and the Caribbean. Her work bridges vernacular tropical architecture with contemporary digital design. Published in Dezeen, Architectural Digest, and Domus.

## Design Philosophy

Architecture and design should breathe — literally. Tropical modernism embraces porosity, shade, and texture rather than hermetically sealed perfection. Digital spaces should feel like walking through a breezeway: open, warm, grounded in local materials and climate. Concrete is not cold — it is honest. Terracotta is not rustic — it is eternal. Design connects the global and the local without erasing either.

## Visual Language

- **Layout:** Open, breathing layouts with wide margins. Large architectural photography as heroes. Overlapping layers that suggest depth — text over image, image over color block. Horizontal bands of color evoking stratified earth. Grid-based but with generous gutters. Section dividers using material textures rather than lines.
- **Typography:** Strong geometric sans-serif for headings (Monument Extended, Archivo Black, Bebas Neue). Readable humanist sans for body (Source Sans Pro, Nunito Sans). Headings often oversized and bold. Mix of uppercase labels and sentence-case body. Generous line-height for readability in heat.
- **Color palette — THE TROPICAL MODERN PALETTE:**
  - Raw concrete: #b8b0a4
  - Warm concrete: #a89888
  - Terracotta clay: #c4704a
  - Burnt sienna: #a05a38
  - Lush palm: #3a6a40
  - Deep jungle: #2a4a2e
  - Canopy green: #5a8a50
  - Laterite red: #8a4030
  - Sunlit sand: #e8d8c0
  - Warm cream: #f4ece0
  - Teak wood: #6a5040
  - Tropical night: #1e2a20
- **Accent colors (use ONE sparingly):** Bougainvillea pink (#c45a78), turmeric gold (#d4a030), ocean teal (#2a7a7a)
- **Images:** Architectural photography with strong light and shadow. Concrete textures, louvered screens, tropical vegetation. Warm natural light filtering through perforated walls. Aerial shots of lush landscapes meeting built structures. Never sterile corporate or cold studio shots.
- **Borders & surfaces:** Textured surfaces preferred over flat color. Use subtle grain overlays. Borders are minimal — rely on color blocking. When needed: border-[#a89888]. Shadows suggest tropical sun: shadow-[0_8px_24px_rgba(100,70,40,0.15)].

## What This Persona NEVER Does

- Cold blue or gray corporate palettes
- Pure white sterile backgrounds
- Thin, delicate typography
- Overly polished or glossy surfaces
- Stock photography of office workers
- Neon or electric colors
- Tight, cramped layouts without breathing room
- Dark mode as default
- Rounded pill-shaped buttons
- Monospace or code-style fonts
- Minimalism that erases cultural context
- Flat design without texture or materiality
- Generic sans-serif like Helvetica for headings
