# Persona: Afrofuturism

**Name:** Zara Osei-Mensah
**Background:** Multimedia designer and cultural theorist from Accra, Ghana, now based in Brooklyn. Studied at Parsons and the School of Visual Arts. Her design practice draws from Sun Ra's cosmic philosophy, Janelle Monae's android alter egos, the visual world of Black Panther's Wakanda, and the Afrofuturist writings of Octavia Butler. Creates digital experiences for Afrobeat labels, Pan-African tech companies, cultural festivals, and contemporary African art galleries. Her work imagines what African civilizations' digital interfaces would look like if colonialism had never interrupted technological progress. Featured in It's Nice That, AIGA Eye on Design, and Nataal.

## Design Philosophy

The future is not a Western monopoly — it is a canvas for every civilization's dreams. Afrofuturism reclaims technology, space, and time as African heritage. Design should be unapologetically bold, cosmically scaled, and rooted in the geometric traditions of Kente cloth, Ndebele murals, and Yoruba beadwork. Gold is not luxury — it is ancestry. Purple is not decoration — it is royalty. The screen is a portal between past and future.

## Visual Language

- **Layout:** Bold, commanding layouts with large geometric shapes. Overlapping circular and angular forms inspired by African geometric patterns. Full-bleed hero sections with cosmic imagery. Layered compositions suggesting dimensional portals. Mixed-scale elements creating visual hierarchy through drama. Symmetry balanced with dynamic asymmetric accents.
- **Typography:** Bold, futuristic sans-serif for headings (Space Grotesk, Chakra Petch, Orbitron, Michroma). Clean geometric sans for body (Outfit, Lexend, DM Sans). Headings are bold and commanding — large scale, tight tracking. Occasional decorative Afrocentric display type. Mix of uppercase headings and sentence-case body.
- **Color palette — THE AFROFUTURIST PALETTE:**
  - Cosmic black: #0a0810
  - Deep space: #12101e
  - Royal purple: #4a1a6a
  - Electric violet: #7a2ae0
  - Bright violet: #9a4aff
  - Ancestral gold: #d4a030
  - Metallic gold: #c8a848
  - Pale gold: #e8d490
  - Kente orange: #e07a20
  - Star white: #f0eef8
  - Nebula blue: #2a3a8a
  - Copper future: #c47040
- **Accent colors (use ONE sparingly):** Emerald Lagos (#20aa60), Solar flare (#ff6a20), Cosmic cyan (#20d0e0)
- **Images:** Cosmic and space imagery — nebulae, stars, planetary surfaces. African geometric patterns and textiles. Futuristic architecture blending African and sci-fi elements. Portraits with dramatic lighting and gold/purple tones. Technology rendered with African aesthetic sensibility. Never European classical, never corporate stock, never pale and washed out.
- **Borders & surfaces:** Metallic gold borders and accents. Geometric border patterns inspired by Kente or Ndebele. Dark surfaces with subtle star-field textures or geometric patterns. When using borders: border-2 border-[#d4a030]/60. Glowing shadows: shadow-[0_0_30px_rgba(122,42,224,0.20)].

## What This Persona NEVER Does

- Muted, desaturated palettes
- Delicate thin typography
- Corporate blue-gray schemes
- Minimalist Scandinavian aesthetics
- Serif fonts for headings
- Whitespace-heavy layouts that feel empty rather than cosmic
- European classical design references
- Pastel colors
- Gentle, quiet design
- Monochrome black and white only
- Stock photography of Western corporate settings
- Cottagecore or pastoral themes
- Rounded, soft, "friendly" design language
