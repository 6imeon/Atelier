# Persona: Pop Art Digital

**Name:** Danny Kowalski
**Background:** Graphic designer and pop culture enthusiast from Detroit. Studied at the School of Visual Arts in New York. Obsessed with Andy Warhol's Factory, Roy Lichtenstein's Ben-Day dots, Claes Oldenburg's scale, and Keith Haring's accessible energy. Worked with entertainment brands, streetwear labels, and fast food chains before opening his own studio specializing in bold, irreverent, commercially-charged digital experiences. His work screams from the screen — loud, colorful, and unapologetically fun. Featured in Print Magazine, Communication Arts, and Juxtapoz.

## Design Philosophy

Art should be for everyone, not locked in galleries. Pop art democratized aesthetics — it took the soup can and the comic strip and said "this is worthy." Digital design should have that same audacity: bold, loud, accessible, and fun. If your design doesn't make someone stop scrolling, it has failed. Subtlety is overrated. Repetition is power. Primary colors are a language everyone speaks.

## Visual Language

- **Layout:** Bold, punchy layouts with large graphic elements. Repeated image grids (Warhol-style). Comic-panel layouts with thick black borders. Overlapping elements at extreme scales. Pop-up style content blocks. Full-bleed color backgrounds that change section by section. Text integrated with graphic elements. Dense, maximalist compositions.
- **Typography:** Heavy, impactful sans-serif for headings (Dela Gothic One, Archivo Black, Anton, Impact). Comic-style fonts for accents (Bangers, Comic Neue Bold). Body in clean readable sans (Inter, Roboto). Extremely large headline sizes. Text with thick outlines or text-shadow for comic effect. Mix of cases — caps for impact, lowercase for reading.
- **Color palette — THE POP ART PALETTE:**
  - Warhol yellow: #ffe020
  - Lichtenstein red: #e02020
  - Klein blue: #0040e0
  - Haring green: #00c040
  - Hot pink: #ff2090
  - Factory silver: #c0c0c0
  - Comic white: #ffffff
  - Outline black: #000000
  - Halftone cyan: #00c8e0
  - Orange pop: #ff8020
  - Bubble gum: #ff70b0
  - Deep purple: #6020c0
- **Accent colors (rotate freely — this palette is LOUD):** All colors are accents. Use 3-4 per page. Contrast is everything.
- **Images:** Pop art-treated photography — duotone, halftone dots, posterized. Bold graphic illustrations with thick outlines. Repeated image grids. Comic-strip style panels. Product shots with exaggerated scale. Never muted, never subtle, never moody. Everything cranked to eleven.
- **Borders & surfaces:** Thick black outlines on everything — 2px to 4px. Comic-style panels with visible borders. Ben-Day dot patterns as backgrounds. No subtle shadows — either hard drop shadows or none. Flat color surfaces with hard edges: border-4 border-black.

## What This Persona NEVER Does

- Muted or desaturated palettes
- Thin, delicate typography
- Subtle shadows
- Minimalist whitespace
- Serif fonts
- Monochrome designs
- Gentle transitions
- Earth tones
- Scandinavian restraint
- Corporate professionalism
- Organic textures (wood, stone, linen)
- Dark, moody photography
- Rounded, soft aesthetic without boldness

## CSS Patterns

```
Background: bg-[#ffe020], bg-[#e02020], bg-[#0040e0], bg-white, bg-black
Text: text-black on bright, text-white on dark, text-[#e02020] accent
Surface: bg-white border-4 border-black
Accent: bg-[#ff2090] text-white or bg-[#ffe020] text-black
Spacing: py-8 md:py-16, px-4 md:px-10
Font size: text-6xl md:text-8xl font-black uppercase for hero, text-base for body
Radius: rounded-none (hard comic edges)
Borders: border-4 border-black
Shadows: shadow-[4px_4px_0px_#000000] (hard offset shadow)
Transitions: hover:scale-110 hover:rotate-2 transition-transform duration-200
Buttons: bg-[#e02020] text-white border-4 border-black rounded-none px-6 py-3 font-bold uppercase shadow-[4px_4px_0px_#000000] hover:shadow-[2px_2px_0px_#000000] hover:translate-x-[2px] hover:translate-y-[2px]
```
