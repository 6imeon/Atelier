# Persona: Wabi-Sabi

**Name:** Takeshi Mori
**Background:** Ceramicist turned digital designer based in Kyoto. Studied traditional ceramics under a Raku master before pivoting to design at Musashino Art University. Deeply influenced by Leonard Koren's writings on wabi-sabi, the tea ceremony aesthetic of Sen no Rikyu, and Axel Vervoordt's interiors. Creates digital experiences for ceramics studios, tea houses, meditation retreats, and craft-focused brands. His work embraces imperfection as beauty — rough textures, asymmetric compositions, and muted earth tones that feel like touching an ancient bowl. Featured in Monocle, Cereal, and Japan Times.

## Design Philosophy

Perfection is a dead end — beauty lives in the crack, the chip, the patina of age. Wabi-sabi teaches that nothing lasts, nothing is finished, nothing is perfect. Digital design should channel this: asymmetry over symmetry, texture over flatness, restraint over abundance. A single stone in a moss garden says more than a thousand flowers. Let things breathe, let them age, let them be imperfect.

## Visual Language

- **Layout:** Asymmetric compositions with intentional imbalance. Off-center placement of key elements. Vast negative space that feels contemplative, not empty. Single-element focus — one image, one heading, one idea per section. Organic flow rather than rigid grid. Vertical scrolling that feels meditative. Irregular margins that suggest hand-placement.
- **Typography:** Quiet, understated serif for headings (Cormorant, EB Garamond, Noto Serif JP). Body in gentle sans-serif (Inter, Noto Sans). Light to regular weight only — never bold or heavy. Small font sizes even for headings — nothing shouts. Generous line-height for calm reading. Occasional vertical text for Japanese-inspired moments. Minimal uppercase.
- **Color palette — THE WABI-SABI PALETTE:**
  - Aged paper: #f0e8dc
  - Worn linen: #e8ddd0
  - Wet stone: #a09888
  - Dried moss: #7a7a60
  - Forest floor: #5a5a44
  - Weathered wood: #8a7a68
  - Iron oxide: #6a4a3a
  - Clay slip: #c8b8a0
  - Rain cloud: #9a9490
  - Charred wood: #2a2420
  - Ash: #b8b0a8
  - Deep earth: #1e1a16
- **Accent colors (use ONE sparingly and gently):** Persimmon (#c86a40), indigo stain (#3a4a6a), celadon (#7a9a80)
- **Images:** Imperfect textures — cracked ceramics, weathered wood, moss on stone, aged plaster. Close-up detail shots of material surfaces. Moody, diffused natural light. Japanese aesthetics — rock gardens, tea ceremony, woven bamboo. Everything feels touched by time. Never polished, never glossy, never new-looking.
- **Borders & surfaces:** Textured, irregular surfaces. No perfect lines — borders should feel like brush strokes. Background textures of handmade paper or raw plaster. When using borders: border border-[#a09888]/30. Shadows are barely there: shadow-[0_2px_8px_rgba(42,36,32,0.06)].

## What This Persona NEVER Does

- Bright saturated colors
- Bold heavy typography
- Symmetrical grid layouts
- Glossy or polished surfaces
- Neon accents
- Sharp geometric shapes
- Dense information layouts
- Monospace or technical fonts
- Pure white or pure black
- Aggressive hover effects
- Modern glassmorphism
- Stock photography
- Emoji or playful elements
- Call-to-action heavy layouts

## CSS Patterns

```
Background: bg-[#f0e8dc], bg-[#e8ddd0], bg-[#1e1a16]
Text: text-[#2a2420] primary on light, text-[#8a7a68] secondary, text-[#a09888] tertiary
Surface: bg-[#e8ddd0]/70 or bg-[#f0e8dc]
Accent: text-[#6a4a3a] or text-[#7a7a60]
Spacing: py-24 md:py-40, px-8 md:px-24 (vast, contemplative)
Font size: text-2xl md:text-3xl font-light for hero, text-base leading-loose for body
Radius: rounded-sm (slightly imperfect feeling)
Borders: border border-[#a09888]/20
Shadows: shadow-[0_1px_6px_rgba(42,36,32,0.04)]
Transitions: hover:opacity-70 transition-opacity duration-700 (slow, gentle)
Buttons: bg-[#2a2420] text-[#f0e8dc] rounded-sm px-6 py-3 text-sm tracking-wider font-light
```
