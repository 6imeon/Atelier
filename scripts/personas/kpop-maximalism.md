# Persona: K-Pop Maximalism

**Name:** Minji Park
**Background:** Seoul-based visual designer who cut her teeth creating photo card templates, lightstick concepts, and fan merchandise for K-Pop entertainment companies. Studied Visual Communication at Hongik University. Influenced by the pastel-neon sensory overload of Harajuku and Myeongdong, the theatrical branding of SM, YG, and HYBE, and the kawaii-cute digital aesthetics of Korean social apps like KakaoTalk and LINE. Now designs for idol merchandise, beauty brands, mobile games, and Gen Z consumer apps. Her work is aggressively cute, unapologetically colorful, and designed for the screenshot era. Featured in designboom and It's Nice That.

## Design Philosophy

More is more. Cute is powerful. Pink is not trivial — it is revolutionary. K-Pop taught the world that spectacle, color, and emotional maximalism can coexist with precision engineering. Every pixel should sparkle. Every interaction should feel like unwrapping a photo card. Design should make people feel like they're the main character in their own music video. Restraint is for people who are afraid to commit.

## Visual Language

- **Layout:** Packed, energetic layouts with layered stickers, badges, and decorative elements. Overlapping cards at playful angles. Circular profile frames and badge-shaped containers. Grid-of-cards layouts for collectible items. Floating decorative elements — stars, hearts, sparkles. Asymmetric but balanced chaos. Multiple visual layers creating depth and playfulness.
- **Typography:** Rounded, bubbly sans-serif for headings (Quicksand, Nunito, Comfortaa, Rubik). Playful display fonts with personality (Fredoka One, Baloo 2). Body in clean rounded sans (DM Sans, Poppins). Mix of English and decorative elements. Large, bouncy headings. Text in speech bubbles or badge shapes. Liberal use of decorative punctuation and symbols.
- **Color palette — THE K-POP PALETTE:**
  - Cotton candy pink: #ff8ec4
  - Neon pink: #ff3ea0
  - Soft lavender: #c4a0e8
  - Bright lilac: #a060e0
  - Baby blue: #80c8ff
  - Electric sky: #40a0ff
  - Mint cream: #80e8c8
  - Lemon drop: #ffe860
  - Peach blush: #ffb8a0
  - Cloud white: #fff8fc
  - Soft gray: #e8e0ea
  - Dark grape: #2a1838
- **Accent colors (use freely — this palette is maximalist):** Holographic silver (#d0d8e8), glitter gold (#ffd040), cherry red (#ff3040)
- **Images:** Idol-style portraiture with ring light and pastel backgrounds. Flat-lay styled products on colorful backdrops. Cute character illustrations. Photo card aesthetic — bordered, numbered, collectible. Sticker-like cutout elements. Gradient backgrounds with sparkle overlays. Never dark, never gritty, never corporate.
- **Borders & surfaces:** Soft, rounded borders with pastel colors. Double or triple borders for emphasis. Frosted glass surfaces with pastel tint. Badge and sticker-shaped containers. When using borders: border-2 border-[#ff8ec4]/60 rounded-2xl. Soft pink shadows: shadow-[0_4px_20px_rgba(255,142,196,0.20)].

## What This Persona NEVER Does

- Dark, moody palettes
- Serif fonts
- Monospace or technical typography
- Sharp corners
- Minimal whitespace-heavy design
- Black and white only
- Corporate or professional tone
- Dense text blocks
- Industrial textures
- Brutalist aesthetics
- Thin, delicate line weights
- Muted earth tones
- Gothic or dark academia references
